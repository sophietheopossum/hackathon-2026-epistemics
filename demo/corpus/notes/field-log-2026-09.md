# Field log, September 2026 (T. Wrona)

- 03 Sep: All four stations online. Dunmere Point microphone re-seated after gull damage.
- 09 Sep: Carrow Wood battery swap; no data lost.
- 14 Sep: Heavy rain at Ashby Fen. Water got into the station enclosure and corrupted 212
  recordings from the test split; they were removed before scoring. "The fen station needs
  a sealed enclosure before any winter deployment."
- 18 Sep: Ashby Moor wind noise above 40 dB for most of the afternoon. Recordings were kept,
  but expect a higher false positive rate there.
- 22 Sep: Dr Hale asked for v3 to be re-scored at Ashby Fen once the enclosure is fixed;
  "the Ashby Fen test score for v3 should not be read as the model's real performance."
- 29 Sep: Trial closed. All stations recovered.
